## 1 · Website design → trust → purchase intention (core mechanism)

- **The effects of website design on purchase intention in online shopping: the
  mediating role of trust and the moderating role of culture.**
  ResearchGate publication 220300397.
  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220300397
  → Trust mediates design's effect on purchase intention and reduces perceived risk.
    Theoretical backbone for the "trust moat vs Temu/Shein" positioning.

- **The Mediating Role of Trust in Website Quality–Purchase Decision Relationships:
  An Empirical Investigation in Digital Commerce.** IJAEM.
  http://ijaem.net (issue_dcp)
  → Website quality → purchase decisions: β = 0.651, p < 0.001. Quantitative proof
    that theme quality moves conversion.

- **The impact of website quality on customer satisfaction and eWOM in online
  purchase intention: the moderating role of gender in risk-taking.**
  Frontiers in Psychology (2022) 13:945707.
  https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.945707/full
  → Information/system/service quality → eWOM → purchase intention. Backs the
    reviews/UGC priority (eWOM = reviews).

## 2 · Visual design & aesthetics

- **Impact of Shopping Website Design on Customer Satisfaction and Loyalty.**
  Sustainability (MDPI, 2023) 15(8):6347.
  https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/15/8/6347
  → Design/usability effects on satisfaction are moderated by trust; when trust is
    high, design's effect strengthens. Build trust signals AND clean design together.

- **Effects of Design Aesthetics on the Perceived Value of a Product.**
  Frontiers in Psychology (2021) 12:670800. (EEG N100/P200 study.)
  https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.670800/full
  → Neurophysiological evidence high aesthetics trigger positive emotion and raise
    perceived value. Justifies fixing contradictory/generic product blocks.

- **Visual design and online shopping experiences: when expertise allows consumers
  to refocus on website attractiveness.** SAGE (2022).
  https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20515707221087627
  → Visual design's influence is moderated by user expertise. Nuance for a mixed
    SA audience.

## 3 · Checkout & cart abandonment (friction points)

- **When Does Perceived Risk Drive Shopping Cart Abandonment? A Meta-Analysis of
  Contextual Heterogeneity in E-Commerce.** MDPI JTAER (2025/2026) 21(8):265.
  https://www.mdpi.com/0718-1876/21/8/265
  → Perceived risk ↔ abandonment r = 0.244; transaction risk > product risk. Supports
    adding trusted SA payment methods (BNPL/Capitec Pay) + secure-checkout signals.

- **Electronic shopping cart abandonment: what do we know and where should we be
  heading?** Electronic Markets (Springer, 2024).
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12525-024-00697-0
  → Systematic review of abandonment drivers — the citable overview.

- **Usability Studies of E-Commerce Checkout Process: A Perspective from Thailand.**
  Springer (2020), LNCS.
  https://link.springer.com/10.1007/978-3-030-50341-3_24
  → Complex/ambiguous checkout lowers conversion. Supports guest checkout + fewer steps.

## 4 · Navigation, information architecture & usability

- **Information Architecture and E-commerce** (Ch. 12, in *Taxonomies*).
  Cambridge University Press (2022).
  https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/taxonomies/information-architecture-and-ecommerce/5BA268FD014F53F41FEA272050825D8E
  → IA principles for navigation, search, product pages AND SEO. Relevant to the
    header/footer nav bug and structured-data work.

- **Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics** (Nielsen & Molich, 1990; Nielsen, 1994) +
  **Understanding the role of heuristics ... on current E-commerce experiences.**
  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/268981807
  → Foundational HCI framework; the "consistency & standards" heuristic is exactly
    what the mixed-link .store nav violated.

- **Usability Evaluation Framework for e-Commerce Websites.** ResearchGate 220709779.
  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220709779
  → Combines user testing + heuristic evaluation + Google Analytics — a reusable
    methodology to measure DriveStack pre/post edits.

## 5 · Product presentation & imagery (fitment-adjacent)

- **Product presentation in the live-streaming context: the effect of consumer
  perceived product value and time pressure on purchase intention.**
  Frontiers in Psychology (2023) 14:1124675.
  https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1124675/full
  → "When consumers cannot touch/try products, product presentation becomes the
    critical clue for judging quality." Academic basis for fitment + accurate specs.

- **Beyond the label itself: how disclosed content shapes consumer responses to
  AI-generated product imagery in E-commerce.**
  Frontiers in Computer Science (2026) 1860932.
  https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2026.1860932/full
  → Aesthetic appeal & social presence → purchase intention & brand trust; disclosure
    matters. Ethics note: if AI imagery is ever used, disclose it.

## How to pull full PDFs

Most of these are open-access (Frontiers, MDPI, Springer OA, PMC/NIH). For paywalled
items (Cambridge, some SAGE), search the title on **Google Scholar**
(scholar.google.com) and use the "All N versions" link to find an open PDF, or check
**unpaywall.org** / the author's ResearchGate. NMU library (learn.mandela.ac.za portal)
also provides institutional access to SAGE/Springer/Taylor & Francis.
